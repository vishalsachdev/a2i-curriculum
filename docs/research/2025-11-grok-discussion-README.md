## Key Findings

### 🎯 Bottom Line

**The proliferation of AI platforms like Grok validates A²I's core philosophy:**

> "Capability is cheap; coherence is scarce."

Multiple capable AI platforms now exist. The valuable skill is orchestrating them coherently for business value—exactly what A²I teaches.

### ✅ Strong Curriculum Alignment

**Our curriculum is well-positioned:**
- We focus on systems orchestration, not mastering single tools
- We teach vendor evaluation and procurement strategy
- We emphasize coherence across multiple capabilities
- We prepare students as AI-augmented professionals

**The multi-platform reality validates this approach.**

### 📈 Recommended Enhancements

**Not major changes—strategic strengthening:**

1. **Multi-Platform Strategy** (Priority 1)
   - Add to A²I 601 (Strategy) and A²I 606 (Governance)
   - Teach students to evaluate and use multiple AI platforms
   - Effort: Medium (6-8 hours development)

2. **Critical AI Evaluation** (Priority 2)
   - Strengthen in A²I 603 (Evidence) and A²I 623 (Evaluation)
   - Systematic comparison across platforms
   - Effort: Medium (4-6 hours per course)

3. **Tool-Agnostic Teaching** (Priority 3)
   - Faculty development on platform-neutral pedagogy
   - Use diverse AI tools in coursework
   - Effort: Medium (faculty workshop + ongoing)

4. **AI Use Policy** (Priority 4)
   - Clear program guidelines for AI platform usage
   - Professional standards development
   - Effort: Low-Medium (policy development)

## Questions Answered

### "Should we change our curriculum philosophy?"
**No.** The multi-platform reality validates our philosophy. Minor enhancements, not major changes.

### "Will this be expensive?"
**No.** ~$2,500 total investment. Most AI platforms offer free/academic access.

### "Will this take a lot of time?"
**Moderate.** ~60 hours development + 1-day workshop. Phased over semester.

### "What if we can't access the original discussion?"
**We've addressed it.** Created framework-based assessment that adds value regardless. Can refine if/when discussion details become available.

### "What if AI platforms keep changing?"
**That's the point.** Teaching platform-agnostic skills means curriculum stays relevant as platforms evolve.

### "How does this compare to other programs?"
**Competitive advantage.** Most programs focus on specific platforms. We teach orchestration across any platform.
