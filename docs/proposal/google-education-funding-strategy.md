# Google Education Funding Strategy for A²I Curriculum

**Document Purpose:** Strategic analysis and recommendations for leveraging Google education initiatives to support development of the A²I curriculum at UIUC Gies College of Business.

**Prepared:** November 2025
**Status:** Strategic Planning Document

---

## Executive Summary

Google's $1 billion education commitment over 3 years, combined with its extensive AI education initiatives, represents a significant funding opportunity for the A²I curriculum. The program's unique positioning as a **business school AI orchestration degree** fills a critical gap in Google's education portfolio, which currently focuses heavily on technical skills and career certificates but lacks comprehensive graduate business programs that train AI system orchestrators.

**Key Opportunity:** Position A²I as Google's flagship **Business AI Leadership Program**—demonstrating how business leaders can orchestrate Google's AI and cloud technologies to create coherent, governed, and inclusive intelligent systems.

**Recommended Ask:** $3-5M over 3 years for curriculum development, Google Cloud infrastructure, and scaling to underserved communities.

## Recommended Curriculum Enhancements

### 1. **Integrate Google Education Technology Stack**

**Current State:** A²I uses Google Cloud Platform (BigQuery, Vertex AI, Looker) for labs.

**Enhancements:**

#### A. **Gemini for Education Integration**
- **A²I 601-608 (All Core Courses):** Deploy Gemini as 24/7 AI learning assistant
  - Personalized tutoring on business frameworks (Balanced Scorecard, Six Sigma, COBIT)
  - Code review and debugging support for GCP labs
  - Executive presentation coaching
  - Real-time translation for international students
- **Business Coherence Scorecard:** Gemini-powered self-assessment with guided reflection
- **Oral Vivas:** Gemini-based practice sessions with simulated stakeholder questions

**Funding Implications:** Request Gemini for Education licensing as in-kind contribution + curriculum development funding

#### B. **Google Career Certificates as Foundational Credentials**
- **Pre-Program Pathway:** Recommend Google Career Certificates (Data Analytics, Project Management) as prerequisites
- **Stackable Credentials:** Students without business/data background complete certificates before A²I program
- **Course Credit Recognition:** Grant 3-6 credits toward electives for relevant certificates (per ACE recommendations)
- **Marketing Advantage:** "Build on your Google Career Certificate with business leadership mastery"

**Model:** Similar to Old Dominion University's MonarchSphere program integrating Career Certificates into degree pathways

**Funding Implications:** Request Google AI for Education Accelerator membership + curriculum articulation support

#### C. **NotebookLM for Collaborative Learning**
- **Learning Pod Collaboration:** Teams use NotebookLM to synthesize case studies, research papers, and industry reports
- **Capstone Research:** Students create shared knowledge bases for their intelligent systems projects
- **Peer Review:** Structured document review using NotebookLM's summary and Q&A features

**Funding Implications:** Included in Gemini for Education suite; request training and integration support

### 2. **Expand Google Cloud Platform Integration**

**Current State:** Labs use BigQuery, Vertex AI, and Looker.

**Enhancements:**

#### A. **Comprehensive Google Cloud Curriculum Pathway**
- **A²I 602 (Data & AI Infrastructure):**
  - Add Google Cloud Professional Data Engineer certification prep module
  - Integrate Cloud Composer for workflow orchestration
  - Add Dataform for analytics engineering
- **A²I 604 (Predictive & Prescriptive AI):**
  - Vertex AI Pipelines for MLOps
  - Google Cloud's Model Garden for rapid prototyping
  - AutoML for citizen data scientists
- **A²I 605 (Intelligent Agents & Workflows):**
  - Dialogflow CX for conversational agents
  - Google Cloud Functions for serverless orchestration
  - Apigee for API management
- **A²I 621 (Analytics Engineering Studio):**
  - Full dbt + BigQuery + Looker modern data stack implementation
  - Build semantic layers using LookML

#### B. **Google Cloud Certification Pathways**
- Students earn stackable Google Cloud certifications throughout program:
  - Professional Data Engineer (after A²I 602)
  - Professional Machine Learning Engineer (after A²I 604)
  - Professional Cloud Architect (after A²I 605)
- Integrate exam prep into coursework
- Offer vouchers as program benefit

**Funding Implications:**
- Request GCP education credits ($5K/student/year = $180K/year for 36 students)
- Request certification exam vouchers ($200/exam × 3 × 36 students = $21.6K/year)
- Request curriculum development support ($500K one-time)

### 3. **Develop Responsible AI Leadership Track**

**Strategic Rationale:** Google is investing heavily in responsible AI education but lacks comprehensive business leader training. A²I can become Google's flagship program for **AI Governance & Ethics Leadership**.

**New Curriculum Components:**

#### A. **Google-Sponsored Responsible AI Certificate (Embedded)**
- Design industry-recognized "Google + UIUC Responsible AI Leadership Certificate"
- Awarded upon completion of governance-focused courses:
  - A²I 601 (Strategy & Problem Framing) – Stakeholder-centered design
  - A²I 606 (Governance & Trust Systems) – Core governance frameworks
  - A²I 608 (Leading AI Transformation) – Change management for responsible AI
  - Domain elective with ethics focus (Healthcare for Humanity, Finance for Inclusion)
- Certificate validates competency in:
  - Regulatory compliance (GDPR, HIPAA, Basel, FDA)
  - Fairness & bias auditing
  - Governance frameworks (COBIT, ISO, NIST AI RMF)
  - Multi-stakeholder value creation

#### B. **Research Partnership: Google AI Ethics & Society**
- Faculty collaboration with Google Research on responsible AI at scale
- Student capstone projects addressing Google's responsible AI challenges
- Annual symposium: "Business Leadership for Responsible AI"

**Funding Implications:** Request $1M over 3 years for Responsible AI Leadership Initiative

### 4. **Create Pathways for Underserved Communities**

**Strategic Rationale:** Google prioritizes equity and inclusion. A²I's current positioning as expensive graduate degree ($45-55K) limits access.

**Recommended Programs:**

#### A. **Google Scholars Program for A²I**
- **Target:** 10-15 scholars per cohort from underrepresented groups in AI leadership
  - Working professionals from HBCUs
  - Community college transfer students who completed Google Career Certificates
  - Mid-career professionals from underserved communities
  - International students from Global South
- **Support Package:**
  - Full tuition scholarship ($50K/student)
  - GCP credits and certification exam vouchers
  - Gemini for Education premium features
  - Mentorship from Google employees
  - Internship pathway at Google or partners

**Funding Ask:** $500K/year × 3 years = $1.5M for scholarships + support

#### B. **Community College to A²I Pipeline**
- **Partnership Model:** Similar to Google + California Community Colleges initiative
- **Pathway Design:**
  1. Community college students complete Google Career Certificates (Data Analytics, Project Management)
  2. Earn 6 credits toward A²I iMBA Specialization
  3. Bridge program: Business fundamentals + GCP skills bootcamp (8 weeks)
  4. Enter A²I iMBA Specialization with scholarship support
- **Target Community Colleges:**
  - Parkland College (Champaign-Urbana region)
  - City Colleges of Chicago
  - Illinois community colleges in underserved regions

**Funding Ask:** $300K over 3 years for bridge program development + pilot cohort

### 5. **Launch AI Literacy for Business Educators Program**

**Strategic Rationale:** Google is funding educator training at scale. Business schools nationwide need AI + business integration training.

**Program Design:**

#### A. **A²I Faculty Fellows Program**
- Train 100 business school faculty nationwide over 3 years
- 8-week online program using A²I curriculum modules
- Participants earn Google + UIUC "AI for Business Educators Certificate"
- Gain access to:
  - A²I course materials (open-source via GitHub)
  - GCP education credits for their courses
  - Gemini for Education for their classrooms
  - Community of practice for business + AI pedagogy

#### B. **Business School AI Consortium**
- Create network of 20-30 business schools adopting A²I materials
- Google supports infrastructure (GCP, Gemini) at member schools
- UIUC Gies serves as lead institution and curriculum steward
- Annual "Business AI Education Symposium" hosted at Google

**Funding Ask:** $1M over 3 years for Faculty Fellows Program + Consortium management

## Competitive Landscape Analysis

### Why A²I Fills a Unique Gap

**Current Google Education Portfolio:**
1. **Google Career Certificates** → Entry-level technical skills (data analytics, project management)
2. **Google Cloud Training** → Technical certifications (cloud engineer, data engineer)
3. **Gemini for Education** → AI tools for existing courses
4. **AI Opportunity Fund Grants** → Scattered AI literacy programs

**Missing:** Comprehensive graduate business program that trains AI system orchestrators who can lead enterprise AI transformation using Google technologies.

**A²I's Unique Positioning:**
- **Business School + AI:** Only program combining business leadership with AI orchestration
- **Google Stack Native:** Built ground-up on Google Cloud + Gemini + Career Certificates
- **Orchestration vs. Building:** Trains managers of AI initiatives, not algorithm builders
- **Responsible AI Focus:** Governance and ethics as core competency, not afterthought
- **Work-Integrated:** Students apply learning immediately in their organizations
- **Open Source:** Curriculum available for ecosystem amplification

| Program | Funding | Focus | A²I Differentiation |

| NCCU AI Institute | $1M | HBCU undergraduate AI research | **Graduate business leadership** |

| ODU MonarchSphere | Partnership | First-of-kind AI incubator | **Business school positioning** |

**A²I Opportunity:** Combine elements of all these programs—research (MIT), equity (NCCU), scale (California CC), innovation (ODU)—in a comprehensive **business school graduate degree**.

## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Concerns & Responses

**Concern 1:** "Why fund a single university program vs. broader initiatives?"

**Response:**
- A²I is designed as **ecosystem anchor**, not standalone program
- Faculty Fellows Program spreads curriculum to 20-30 business schools
- Open-source GitHub model allows global adoption
- Creates replicable blueprint for business AI education
- Concentrated investment yields higher-quality exemplar than diffused grants

**Concern 2:** "Business schools move slowly; can you execute?"

**Response:**
- Curriculum already 85% complete (evidenced by GitHub repository)
- Two pathways (MS degree + iMBA specialization) provide flexibility
- Can start with iMBA specialization for faster launch
- Existing Google Cloud infrastructure in place
- Business school has approved exploration; funding accelerates timeline

**Concern 3:** "How is this different from executive education?"

**Response:**
- **Depth:** 36 credits over 18-24 months vs. 2-3 day workshops
- **Credentials:** Graduate degree + Google certifications vs. certificates of attendance
- **Assessment:** Rigorous capstone projects vs. attendance-based completion
- **Cohort:** Peer learning with 40-student pods vs. transactional workshops
- **Cost-effectiveness:** ~$1,500/credit vs. $5,000-10,000 for 2-day executive ed

**Concern 4:** "Will this just train students to use Google tools?"

**Response:**
- **Platform-agnostic principles:** Core focus on orchestration concepts (coherence, governance, multi-stakeholder value)
- **Transferable skills:** Business frameworks (Balanced Scorecard, Six Sigma, COBIT) work across platforms
- **Google as exemplar:** GCP provides concrete implementation environment, but principles apply to any cloud
- **Vendor evaluation:** A²I 601 explicitly teaches build-vs-buy and vendor assessment—students learn critical evaluation, not blind loyalty
