# Google Education Funding: Executive Summary

**Prepared:** November 2025
**For:** Initial conversations with Google education teams and Gies leadership

---

## The Opportunity

**Google's $1B education commitment** over 3 years creates a strategic funding opportunity for A²I. Position the program as Google's flagship **Business AI Leadership Program**—demonstrating how business leaders orchestrate Google's technology stack to create coherent, governed, and inclusive intelligent systems.

## The Gap We Fill

**Google's Current Education Portfolio:**
- ✅ Google Career Certificates → Entry-level technical skills
- ✅ Google Cloud Training → Technical certifications
- ✅ Gemini for Education → AI tools for existing courses
- ✅ AI Opportunity Fund → Scattered AI literacy programs
- ❌ **Comprehensive graduate business program for AI system orchestrators**

**A²I Unique Positioning:**
- **Business school graduate degree** (not technical bootcamp)
- **AI orchestration** (not algorithm building)
- **Responsible AI governance** (not just tools)
- **Built entirely on Google stack** (Cloud, Gemini, Career Certificates)

## The Ask: $3-5M Over 3 Years

| Initiative | Amount | Impact |
|-----------|---------|--------|
| **Google Technology Integration** | $850K | Showcase GCP + Gemini + Career Certificates |
| **Responsible AI Leadership Certificate** | $1M | Flagship governance program |
| **Google Scholars Program** | $1.5M | 45 scholarships for underserved communities |
| **Community College Pipeline** | $300K | Access pathway from Google Career Certificates |
| **Faculty Fellows Program** | $1M | Train 100 business school educators nationwide |
| **Evaluation & Research** | $300K | Impact measurement |
| **Total** | **$5M** | Comprehensive ecosystem initiative |

## Value Proposition for Google

**Why Fund A²I?**

1. **Talent Pipeline:** Graduates buy and implement Google Cloud enterprise solutions
2. **Market Positioning:** Own "business AI education" vs. competitors' technical focus
3. **Thought Leadership:** Advance responsible AI governance at scale
4. **Equity & Inclusion:** Reach underserved communities (HBCUs, community colleges)
5. **Ecosystem Building:** Open-source curriculum spreads to 50+ business schools
6. **Technology Showcase:** Comprehensive integration of Google education stack

## Success Metrics (Years 1-3)

- **120 students** in A²I program (3 cohorts)
- **45 Google Scholars** from underserved communities
- **100 business school educators** trained via Faculty Fellows Program
- **80%** of students earn ≥1 Google Cloud certification
- **50+ institutions** adopt curriculum via open-source model
- **90%** placement in AI leadership roles within 6 months

## Curriculum Enhancements Leveraging Google

### 1. Gemini for Education Integration
- 24/7 AI learning assistant for all courses
- Personalized tutoring on business frameworks
- Executive presentation coaching
- Real-time translation for international students

### 2. Google Career Certificates Recognition
- Stackable credentials pathway (certificates → A²I degree)
- 3-6 credit hours for relevant certificates
- Community college bridge program

### 3. Comprehensive Google Cloud Curriculum
- **A²I 602:** BigQuery, Dataform, Cloud Composer, Looker
- **A²I 604:** Vertex AI, AutoML, Model Garden, MLOps
- **A²I 605:** Dialogflow CX, Cloud Functions, Apigee
- **Labs:** dbt + BigQuery + Looker modern data stack
- **Certifications:** Data Engineer, ML Engineer, Cloud Architect pathways

### 4. Responsible AI Leadership Certificate (Google-Sponsored)
- Industry-recognized governance certification
- Core courses: Strategy, Governance, Transformation, Domain Ethics
- Validates: Regulatory compliance, fairness auditing, COBIT/ISO frameworks

### 5. Faculty Fellows Program
- Train business school educators nationwide
- Spread Google Cloud + Gemini adoption
- Create business school AI consortium (20-30 schools)
- Annual "Business AI Education Symposium"

- **Market Demand:** Organizations need AI leadership, not just technical talent
